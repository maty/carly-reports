● The basics

What is an OBD Scanner?

OBD stands for on-board diagnostics — the computer system every modern car uses to monitor itself. When something goes wrong, your car writes down exactly what and where. A scanner is simply the key that unlocks those notes.

The port that's already in your car

Every car has a 16-pin socket under the dash

It's been standard on basically every car since the late '90s. That trapezoid-shaped socket is the OBD-II port — the exact same one a workshop plugs into when you drop your car off.

Where to look

In the driver's footwell, above the pedals

Under or just beside the steering column

Behind a small flip-down cover near the fuse panel

Almost always within arm's reach of the steering wheel

Look for this shape. If you can find it, Carly works — no wiring, no adapters, no tools.

A warning light is a headline, not the story

The check-engine light is one bulb covering hundreds of possible faults. Behind it, your car has already stored a specific code — say P0301, a misfire on cylinder one. The dashboard won't show it. A scanner will.

Carly is the scanner plus the translator

A basic code reader hands you "P0301" and leaves. Carly connects over Bluetooth and tells you what it means, how urgent it is, and step by step instructions on how to fix it yourself.

● FAQ

Questions from first-timers

Do I need any mechanical knowledge to use it?+

None. Carly is built for everyday drivers, not mechanics. Every result comes with an explanation of what it means and what to do next.

Is this going to damage my car or void the warranty?+

Reading diagnostics is passive — it's the same port and the same data a workshop reads. Customization changes are reversible, and Carly warns you before anything that alters a setting.

Where exactly is my OBD-II port?+

Within about three feet of the steering wheel — usually in the driver's footwell, under the dashboard, or behind a small cover near the fuse panel. It's a 16-pin trapezoid socket. The app shows the location for your specific model.

How is this different from a cheap code reader?+

Generic readers show engine codes only, as raw numbers. Carly reads manufacturer-specific systems across the whole car and explains each result, with maintenance resets and customization on top.
