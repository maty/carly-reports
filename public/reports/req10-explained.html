What we're asking dev to build

Five requirements — each one is what/why/how, not just a spec line.

REQ-10.1

Add product schema to all 20 lp-obd-* pages

What
One templated block of Product / Offer / AggregateRating schema, filled in per brand and country.
Why
Right now only 1 of these 20 pages has this — and it's the only one getting any AI-referred traffic.
Check first
Confirm with dev whether all 20 pages share one Elementor template, or whether UK/US variants need a second version.
REQ-10.2

Same fix for the 12 active lp-shopping-* pages

What
Same schema pattern as REQ-10.1, adjusted for a general offer rather than one brand.
Why
Same gap, same opportunity, different template family.
REQ-10.3

Answer the question on the page itself, not just in schema

What
A visible content block: "What can I diagnose and code on my {{BRAND}} with Carly?" — listing the actual systems supported.
Why
Schema alone doesn't make a page a good AI answer. The copy has to actually answer what someone would ask ChatGPT.
Bonus
This text can also back an optional FAQ schema entry — no downside, since Google retired the FAQ rich result but AI crawlers still read the markup.
REQ-10.4

Clean up three pages before investing in them

What
/lp-shopping-en-uk-test, /lp-polish-shopping, /lp-ro-shopping — all near 0% conversion.
Why
This looks like stale A/B tests or broken tracking, not real underperformance.
Next step
Confirm with Christopher whether these are still linked from live campaigns before spending schema work on them.
REQ-10.5

Measure this monthly, not weekly

What
Track AI-sourced sessions on /lp-* pages, split by family, on a monthly cadence.
Why
35 sessions over 8 weeks is too little to read week to week — it's mostly noise at this volume.
Revisit
Once this page set hits ~150+ AI sessions/month, start tracking conversion rate on that subset too.

What this REQ does not cover

  • /obd-codes/* and /community/* — these get real AI traffic but convert near 0%. Different problem: visibility, not revenue. Tracked separately.
  • support.mycarly.com tracking gap — affects how we measure citations, not the /lp-* pages themselves. Tracked separately.
  • New landing pages — this REQ only improves what already exists.
