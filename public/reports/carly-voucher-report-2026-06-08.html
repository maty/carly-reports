Key Insights
🚀
DE is pulling double duty — and winning.
With 8 ranking keywords, positions #1–#3 across high-intent terms, and org traffic growing from 50 → 244 in 4 months, DE is the only market where SEO is becoming a genuine paid-traffic offset. CPA of $9.88 is 15% better than EN.
⚠️
EN page is a multi-market catch-all — and it's costing efficiency.
Campaigns from DE, US, UK, Worldwide, and even FR are all landing on the single EN voucher URL. This creates attribution noise, inflates CPA ($12.77 vs $9.88 for DE), and makes SEO targeting impossible — no single geo to optimize for.
📈
FR has paid intent but almost no organic floor.
Only 1 Ahrefs keyword tracked (#5 for "code promo carly"), yet paid CPA is $15.08 and the page only exists in Ahrefs data since ~April. FR is the clearest case for a content + link-building investment to reduce paid dependency.
🔴
ES is effectively invisible.
Zero paid campaigns targeting the ES URL, zero Ahrefs keyword visibility, and only 16 GA4 sessions in 30 days (5 from organic). The page exists but receives no intentional traffic from either channel.
📊
Cross-network dominates EN and DE — Direct dominates conversions anomalously.
The EN Direct channel shows 129 sessions but 118 conversions (91% CVR) — almost certainly a GA4 attribution artifact from Performance Max assisted clicks misclassified as Direct. DE Direct shows a more realistic 51% CVR (32/63).
💡
DE organic engagement quality exceeds paid.
Organic Search avg session duration in DE is 140.6s vs 128s for Paid Search. Bounce rate for organic is 11.6% vs 3.4% for paid. Users arriving organically are genuinely intent-qualified — a strong signal to accelerate DE SEO investment.
Top 3 Recommendations
1
Fix EN URL fragmentation — dedicate campaigns to specific geos
Audit all campaigns currently landing on the EN voucher URL. UK campaigns should target the EN URL exclusively; DE/US/FR campaigns should be redirected to their respective localized pages. This will improve Quality Score, reduce wasted spend on cross-geo mismatches, and make SEO geo-targeting viable for the EN page (GB market). Expected CPA improvement: 10–20%.
2
Launch FR organic program — target "bon de réduction carly" cluster
The FR page has zero organic infrastructure. "Code promo carly" (150 vol, pos #5) is a start but the full cluster — "réduction carly", "promo carly OBD", "carly bon plan" — is unaddressed. Prioritize internal linking from FR category pages, 2–3 editorial backlinks from FR automotive blogs, and on-page optimization. At DE's CPA ($9.88), displacing 50 paid FR sessions with organic saves ~$500/month.
3
Activate ES — or deprecate the page
The ES voucher page has had zero paid campaigns in 30 days and no Ahrefs traction. Either (a) activate a dedicated ES brand campaign and run it for 60 days to validate demand, or (b) 301-redirect the ES URL to the main shop and stop indexing a near-orphan page. Leaving it as-is dilutes crawl budget and creates a dead-end for any ES users who find it organically.
