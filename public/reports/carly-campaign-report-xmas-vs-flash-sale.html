Why XMAS had +100% more sessions than Flash Sale

The XMAS period generated 338.8K GA4 sessions vs 169.8K during the April Flash Sale — a gap of 169.0K sessions. Three factors explain this: 1) Paid media scale — XMAS deployed €64,819 in Google Ads vs €30,372 in April, plus 6.5M Demand Gen impressions vs 3.1M in April. 2) Paid social — Facebook Ads brought 86.2K sessions in XMAS vs 31.9K in April (-63%), TikTok delivered 21.5K vs 6.3K (-71%). 3) Seasonal organic lift — Google Organic delivered 82.7K XMAS sessions vs 49.9K in April (-40%), driven by peak pre-Christmas car maintenance searches.

Flash Sale achieved better ROAS at lower volume

Despite running at roughly half the session volume, the April Flash Sale achieved a ROAS of 5.37x vs 4.76x at XMAS (+12.8%). The 20% discount drove a stronger purchase intent: the GA4 transaction rate was 2.43% vs 2.51% during XMAS. AOV was €74.25 vs €73.14 — slightly lower due to the discount, but overall revenue efficiency per euro spent was higher in April.

Demand Gen — -52% impressions

XMAS pushed 6.5M Demand Gen impressions (YouTube + Display) at €33,089 spend. April ran just 3.1M at €11,868. XMAS Demand Gen ROAS was 0.69x vs 0.39x in April — both sub-1x, confirming Demand Gen drives awareness and assist conversions, not direct ROAS.

Brand PMax — -43% impressions

Brand Performance Max dropped from 1.1M in XMAS to 620.4K in April. Both periods maintained consistent ROAS (~5x), confirming stable brand demand regardless of season. The reduction reflects lower retargeting budgets in April rather than weakened brand intent.

Acq PMax nearly off in Flash Sale (-91% impressions)

Acq PMax generated 860.3K XMAS impressions vs just 78.7K in April. The algorithm scaled back aggressively when the campaign budget or ROAS targets weren't met. Meanwhile Acq SEM grew +31%, suggesting the 20% Flash Sale offer drove stronger keyword-level bidding opportunities.

What Is an Impression in Google Ads?

An impression is counted each time an ad is displayed to a user — but what "displayed" means differs by campaign type:

Brand SEM / Acq SEM

A user actively typed a search query and your ad appeared in results. High purchase intent. CTR and CPA are most meaningful here.

Brand PMax / Acq PMax

Google's algorithm places ads across Search, Shopping, Display, Gmail, and YouTube. Intent varies — Shopping is high-intent, Display is awareness. High volume but mixed quality.

Demand Gen (YouTube)

An impression = a video ad was served, not necessarily watched. Skippable ads count even if skipped after 5 seconds. This explains 6.5M XMAS Demand Gen impressions at sub-1x ROAS — pure awareness spend.

⚠️ When comparing XMAS vs Flash Sale impression totals, note that XMAS allocated 75% of all impressions to Demand Gen vs 78% in April. Raw impression volume comparisons are therefore misleading — XMAS skewed heavily toward reach/awareness, while the Flash Sale was concentrated in higher-intent performance channels.
