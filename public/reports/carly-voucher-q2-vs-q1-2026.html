Key Insights — Q2 vs Q1
🚀
DE scaled 4x and became more efficient — the only market to improve CPA while massively increasing volume.
DE went from €1,016 spend / 101 conv (Q1) to €3,742 spend / 411 conv (Q2) — a 4.1x revenue proxy with CPA improving from €10.02 to €9.11. This is textbook efficient scaling. The organic side tracked proportionally: 81→302 sessions (+273%), suggesting organic authority is building alongside paid investment. DE is the template for what FR could become in Q3.
⚠️
EN spend grew 54% but conversions only 39% — a CPA degradation of 11% driven by two specific campaigns.
The "Acq EN UK - Car Brands - No Remarketing" campaign registered a CPA of €2,998 in Q2 (€27.42 spend for 0.009 conversions — essentially €27 with zero result). Brand EN US went from €13.82 → €21.25 CPA. These two campaigns together wasted ~€440 in Q2 with negligible return, pulling the blended CPA from what should be sub-€9 up to €11.49.
🇫🇷
FR went from near-zero to 231 paid clicks and €354 spend in Q2 — the biggest relative growth story.
Q1: 44 clicks, €55 spend, 10.5 conversions at €5.25 CPA. Q2: 231 clicks, €355 spend, 54 conversions at €6.55 CPA. The CPA rose 25% (expected when scaling from a small base) but remains best portfolio CPA. Organic sessions also tripled (8→30), confirming growing page authority. The FR ramp in Q2 explains why it changed from "Opportunity" (future potential) to "Scaling" (actively growing).
🇪🇸
ES organic grew 225% with zero paid investment — but volumes are still too small to draw conclusions.
Organic sessions went from 8 (Q1) to 26 (Q2). GA4 shows 12 paid search sessions in Q2 with €0 Google Ads spend — possibly from non-Carly paid channels or attribution errors. With zero Ahrefs keyword rankings, this is navigational traffic, not search-intent capture. ES remains an undeveloped market; the organic signal is encouraging but insufficient for paid investment without keyword demand validation first.
📊
Account daily spend more than tripled Q1→Q2 (€32.31 → €88.74/day) while blended CPA held — structurally positive.
Total paid conversions grew from 289 (Q1, 90 days) to 711 (Q2, 78 days) — that's 3.21 conv/day → 9.12 conv/day, a +184% per-day improvement. The blended CPA barely moved (€10.05 → €9.73). This means the Q2 budget increase was well-absorbed across DE and FR. The only drag is EN, where campaign fragmentation is creating CPA inflation on a market that should be performing similarly to DE.
Top 3 Actions for Q3
01
Pause Acq EN UK Car Brands + Brand EN US immediately
Acq EN UK Car Brands delivered a €2,998 CPA in Q2 — €27 spend for effectively 0 conversions. Brand EN US went from €13.82 → €21.25 CPA. Combined waste: ~€440 in Q2. Pausing both and reallocating to Brand EN Worldwide (€7.35 CPA) would immediately improve EN blended CPA from €11.49 to sub-€9, bringing it in line with DE performance. Zero scale loss — these campaigns contribute <5% of EN volume.
Impact: High · Action: Now
02
Replicate DE scaling model in FR for Q3
DE proved you can 4x spend while improving CPA. FR is at exactly the stage DE was in Q1 — single efficient campaign, €6.55 CPA, strong organic co-growth. Increase FR daily budget from ~€4.55/day to €15-20/day in Q3. Target: 400-500 FR conversions in Q3 at under €8 CPA. Also push "code promo carly" (pos. 5) to top-3 with internal linking and 2-3 FR editorial links — organic will reduce blended CPA as paid scales.
Impact: High · Q3 priority
03
Audit EN campaign structure before Q3 budget renewal
EN has 12+ active campaigns on one URL, 4 Brand EN PMax variants, and cross-contamination from DE/FR campaigns. Before increasing EN budget in Q3, consolidate: keep Brand EN UK + Brand EN Worldwide, pause CPA Test (redundant), kill US and Acq variants. Set negative audience exclusions between campaigns. EN Q1 had better CPA (€10.35) than Q2 (€11.49) — the deterioration is structural, not market-driven, and fixable without reducing volume.
Impact: Medium · Pre-Q3
