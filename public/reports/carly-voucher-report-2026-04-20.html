Key insights

🏆
DE is the portfolio anchor.802 paid clicks, €1,418 spend, CPA €9.52 — combined with top-3 organic positions on "carly rabatt code", "carly 25% rabatt", and "mycarly gutschein". Strongest paid + organic cohesion of any market.
⚡
FR is the most efficient market.€6.40 CPA — 33% below DE and 46% below EN — on just €119 of spend. Budget is the bottleneck, not performance.
🧩
EN URL is serving 15+ non-EN campaigns.The EN voucher page receives paid traffic from DE, FR, BE, AT, CH, NL, NO, SE, ES and Worldwide campaigns — a clear geo/language routing issue inflating EN clicks and lowering conversion quality.
🕳️
ES has no meaningful presence.1 paid click · €4.74 spend · 8 organic sessions · zero Ahrefs-tracked keywords. The page is effectively dormant in its target market.
📉
Organic trails paid by 28%.189 organic sessions vs 264 paid-search sessions across the four URLs. Without paid support, voucher traffic would collapse.
🚪
EN Direct channel shows high bounce.124 Direct sessions at 74.2% bounce / 29.5s duration — suggests pasted voucher-code URLs landing users cold. High intent in the URL, low engagement on the page.
📈
EN organic ranking is climbing.Ahrefs monthly organic traffic for the EN URL: 1 → 4 → 17. "carly discount code" (vol 450) at position 9 is the next win — moving to top 3 materially lifts this URL.
⚖️
Organic has lower bounce than paid.DE organic bounce 13.0% vs DE Cross-network 26.2%. EN organic 8.1% vs EN Direct 74.2%. Organic voucher visitors are warmer and more engaged.

Top 3 recommendations

01

Fix the EN URL geo-routing leak

The EN voucher page is receiving paid traffic from DE, FR, BE, AT, CH, NL, NO and SE campaigns. Re-point these campaigns to their language-specific voucher URLs (DE → /de/..., FR → /fr/...). Expected outcome: lower EN CPA closer to DE's €9.52 and better conversion quality per market.

02

Scale FR paid spend

FR delivers the lowest CPA in the portfolio (€6.40) but absorbs only €119 in 30 days. Increase daily budget on "Brand_[Performance Max] FR - Shop" by 3–5× and watch CPA elasticity. Even at 2× CPA, FR would remain more efficient than EN.

03

Revive or retire ES

ES has 1 paid click, 8 organic sessions, and zero Ahrefs visibility. Two-path decision: (a) invest in ES SEO — target "código promocional carly", "descuento carly obd" and add a dedicated ES PMax campaign; or (b) de-index the ES voucher page and redirect to the ES product page. Status quo wastes crawl budget and internal equity.
