Impressions by type: Demand Gen dominates both campaigns (>73% of total impressions), as expected for video/display upper-funnel activity. Total Deal Days impressions (3.83M) were slightly below Flash Sale (3.99M) — a -4.1% difference — suggesting similar overall reach despite the extra days.
What are impressions? An impression is counted each time an ad is shown to a user. For Search ads, this means a user’s query triggered and displayed the ad in results. For Performance Max / Shopping, impressions span Google Search, Shopping, YouTube, Display, Gmail, and Maps. For Demand Gen, impressions represent video/display views on YouTube, Discover, and Gmail. A single user can generate multiple impressions by performing multiple searches or visiting multiple pages.
