Key Insights
🇩🇪
DE has two overlapping Brand PMax campaigns bidding on the same page — €1,350 combined spend, different CPAs.
"Brand PMax DE - Shop" costs €14.42 CPA for 309 clicks, while "Brand PMax DE" delivers €6.56 CPA for 348 clicks on the identical URL. These campaigns are cannibalizing each other's bids. Consolidating or separating by audience segment could reduce DE CPA below €8. Additionally, the DE organic page ranks #1-3 for 5 keywords — those organic conversions are currently invisible to campaign attribution.
⚠️
EN has severe campaign fragmentation: CPA range €6.38–€88.95 across 12 campaigns on a single URL.
The Acq PMax EN Worldwide campaign spent €22 for 0.25 conversions (€88.95 CPA). Brand EN US spent €133 for 3.6 conversions (€36.62 CPA). Meanwhile Brand EN Worldwide delivers €7.35 CPA. DE and FR campaigns are also routing clicks to the EN URL (€49 combined spend with minimal conversions). Without budget caps per campaign, underperformers drain the account silently.
🇫🇷
FR is the most efficient market at €7.07 CPA but receives only 5% of total spend — significant upside untapped.
Brand PMax FR - Shop alone delivers €6.95 CPA with 87 clicks and €133 spend. The organic search channel on FR also shows 0% bounce rate and 115s avg session duration — users landing organically are highly engaged. "code promo carly" sits at pos. 5 (vol 150) with room to push to top-3. A 3x budget test on FR could yield 50-60 additional conversions at under €8 CPA.
🎯
Direct traffic in EN generates 87.9% key event rate (117 events from 133 sessions) — far above paid search's 22.8%.
GA4 "conversions" are key events, not necessarily purchases. Direct users hitting the voucher page are highly intentional — likely brand loyalists or returning customers using the page as a discount utility. This suggests the EN page functions as a retention/loyalty tool, not just an acquisition landing page. Attribution models that only count last-click paid are understating the voucher page's actual commercial role.
🇪🇸
ES exists in GA4 (21 sessions, 4 key events, 0% bounce on organic) but has zero paid investment and no Ahrefs rankings.
The 5 organic sessions in GA4 with strong engagement metrics (141.9s avg duration, 0% bounce) suggest the page is indexed and users who find it are interested. The gap: no keyword rankings in Ahrefs means discoverability is essentially zero without branded search. Before paid investment, audit for indexation, run Keywords Explorer on "código descuento carly" and related terms to confirm search demand exists at scale in ES.
📊
Organic is only 12.1% of total traffic across 4 voucher pages — paid dependency is high and unsustainable long-term.
185 organic sessions vs 1,529 total. DE has the best organic-to-paid ratio (110 organic / 742 total = 14.8%) driven by 7 keywords in top-4 positions. EN sits at 8.8% organic share despite having the primary keyword at pos. 10. If "carly discount code" moved to pos. 3 (realistic), estimated organic traffic for EN would increase by ~180 visits/month, equivalent to €380+ in avoided paid spend at current CPA.
