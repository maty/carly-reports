Root Cause Analysis & Funnel Mechanism
Google Ads on-platform metrics remained stable throughout the period. DE Defensive maintained CTR 30–33%, CPC €0.035–0.060, impressions 2,200–4,300/day. The drop is not a bidding, quality score, or competition issue — it is a demand-side effect caused by the removal of upper-funnel Meta traffic.
1

Meta Suspension (May 3–9)

Meta Ads account suspended for 6 days. Facebook PPC sessions in GA4 dropped from ~900/day to <50/day. Total Meta sessions fell to 143–211/day (–86% vs. baseline of ~1,280/day).

2

Awareness Pipeline Collapse

Meta generates the upper-funnel audience that later searches for the brand on Google. With 6 days of near-zero Meta exposure, no new audience entered the branded search pipeline for those days.

3

5–7 Day Lag Effect

Brand Search conversions start declining May 10–11 — exactly 5–7 days after the suspension started (May 3–4). This matches the typical consideration-to-search cycle for this product.

4

CPA Cascade

Brand Defensive campaigns are the lowest-CPA campaigns in the account (€2.11/day avg). As their conversion volume drops, their weight in the account blended CPA decreases, pulling the total account CPA from €18.77 → €26.29 (+40%).

5

Recovery Timing

Meta sessions recovered to baseline from May 10. However, Brand Search continues declining through May 16 because the new audience re-entering the funnel from May 10 needs another 5–7 days to convert. Expected Brand Search recovery: May 20–25.

6

Post-Recovery: Watch Meta Volume

May 15–17 Meta sessions (1,140–1,371/day) are still slightly below the pre-suspension peak. Ensure Meta spend is fully restored — any further dip will extend the Brand Search recovery timeline.
