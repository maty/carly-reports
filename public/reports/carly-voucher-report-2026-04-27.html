Key Insights
🏆
DE dominates both paid volume and organic ranking. With 792 paid clicks (57% of all clicks), a #2 ranking for "carly gutschein" (500 vol), and 111 organic sessions, DE is the only market with a functioning paid + SEO flywheel. However, Ahrefs shows a traffic drop from 50 to 10 month-over-month — worth monitoring for ranking stability.
💰
FR has the best CPA at €6.21 — 40% cheaper than EN. At only €113 spend and 18.2 conversions, France is severely underfunded relative to its efficiency. The paid search sessions also show 242s average duration — far exceeding DE (108s) and EN (114s), suggesting high intent visitors.
⚠️
EN campaigns are fragmented across 8 different campaigns with conflicting CPAs. The DE Shop campaign is sending €63 to the EN URL. CPAs range from €1.48 (FR Shop — misattributed) to €30.61 (DE brand variant), indicating campaign structure issues. The EN page ranks #1 for "carly discount code" (450 vol) in Ahrefs but reports 0 estimated traffic — a possible featured snippet cannibalization or data lag.
🚨
ES is fully absent from paid and has near-zero organic presence. 23 total sessions, no Ahrefs keywords, no paid campaigns detected, and a 46% organic bounce rate. Despite Carly having an ES page, no budget allocation exists, and SEO traction is zero. This is either a deliberate market deprioritization or a coverage gap.
📊
Cross-network (PMax) is the dominant driver in DE and FR. PMax accounts for 55.6% of DE sessions and 60% of FR sessions. Combined, PMax drives ~1,058 paid sessions across all markets vs 187 organic — making these pages 85% paid-dependent for traffic.
🔍
EN organic quality is excellent but volume is low. The EN page's organic visitors have a 5.5% bounce rate and 2m37s average duration — the best engagement metrics across all markets. With a #1 ranking for "carly discount code" and a growing Ahrefs trend (1→3→8 over 3 months), this page has strong organic momentum that is currently overshadowed by paid volume.
Top 3 Recommendations
01
Scale FR Paid Budget
FR converts at €6.21 CPA — the most efficient market by a wide margin. With only €113 spend driving 18.2 conversions, increasing the FR budget 3–5× would likely maintain sub-€8 CPA based on current efficiency. Ahrefs shows no organic keywords ranking yet, so paid is the only lever available short-term. Target a monthly budget of €400–500 as a test.
02
Consolidate EN Campaign Structure
Eight separate campaigns are targeting the EN voucher URL, including DE and FR brand campaigns spilling traffic across markets. Consolidate EN-specific traffic into 2–3 clearly scoped campaigns (UK, US, Worldwide) with proper geo-exclusions to prevent cross-market attribution bleed. The current setup inflates EN's apparent CPA and makes optimization difficult.
03
Build ES SEO Foundation
With 0 Ahrefs keywords and 0 paid spend, the ES market is invisible. Before launching paid campaigns in Spain, invest in on-page SEO: target "cupón descuento Carly" and "código promocional Carly" (validate volumes), build internal links from the ES homepage and product pages, and get a handful of Spanish automotive blogs to link to the ES voucher page. A paid campaign on an unranked page with a 46% bounce rate will have low Quality Scores and high CPCs.
