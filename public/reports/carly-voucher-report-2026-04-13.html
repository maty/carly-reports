Key Insights
🇩🇪
DE is the clear revenue engine — but overexposed to paid. DE drives 747 paid clicks and €1,477 in spend with a €9.91 CPA. Organic sits at 114 sessions with position #2 for "carly gutschein" (vol 500). Heavy reliance on Performance Max (Brand_[PMax] DE) means organic is underpinning a fraction of what paid carries.
🔀
EN page has campaign sprawl across 10+ geos pointing at one URL. Campaigns from DE, FR, BE, AT, UK, US, Worldwide all route to the same EN page. This inflates CPA to €11.73 and fragments geo relevance. The page ranks #1 in Ahrefs for "carly discount code" (vol 450) but organic traffic conversion is low (4 GA4 conversions from 61 sessions).
💶
FR delivers the best CPA at €5.76 on a shoestring budget. With only €99 spend and 80 clicks, FR returns 17.2 conversions — the most efficient market. Zero organic bounce rate and 2m 17s avg session duration signal high intent. This market is under-invested relative to its unit economics.
🇪🇸
ES is effectively absent — 1 paid click, no Ahrefs keyword data. The ES page exists but has no meaningful paid investment (1 click, €4.74 spend) and zero tracked organic rankings. GA4 shows 13 organic sessions, likely from long-tail untracked terms. No competitive paid activity observed from Carly's account for this market.
📈
EN organic traffic tripling month-over-month per Ahrefs. Ahrefs metrics history shows org_traffic on the EN URL growing: 1→4→9 across the last 3 recorded months. Combined with a #1 ranking for "carly discount code" (vol 450), the EN page is building organic momentum that could reduce paid dependency.
🛒
Cross-network (Performance Max) dominates DE and EN paid traffic. Cross-network accounts for 53% of DE sessions (424) and 42% of EN sessions (296). These high-converting sources (184 and 129 GA4 conversions respectively) indicate PMax is working well but obscures campaign-level attribution — making SEO-vs-Paid apples-to-apples comparison difficult.
⚠️
FR and ES pages have no Ahrefs keyword footprint — pure brand dependency. Both FR and ES returned zero organic keywords in Ahrefs. The FR page has 10 GA4 organic sessions but these are likely navigational/brand searches not captured at URL-level in Ahrefs yet. Content freshness or internal linking may be insufficient to index properly.
🎯
DE paid CPC for "carly gutschein" sits at €42 — high overlap cost. Bidding on branded voucher terms while organically ranking #2 creates self-competition. With 50 Ahrefs-estimated organic visits/month from this keyword alone, incrementality of paid spend here is questionable. An holdout test could quantify true paid lift.
Top 3 Recommendations
Rec 01
Scale FR paid investment — it's your most efficient market
FR's €5.76 CPA is 44% below DE's and 51% below EN's. With only €99 spend and 80 clicks in 30 days, the budget ceiling has not been tested. Allocate a dedicated Performance Max campaign for FR (currently it receives spill from Brand_[PMax] FR - Shop and Brand_[PMax] BE - Shop only). Target €500–€1,000/month to validate whether the CPA holds at volume before expanding to the FR-specific SEM campaigns that are currently absent.
Rec 02
Fix EN campaign fragmentation — consolidate geo targeting
11+ campaigns across UK, US, DE, FR, BE, AT, and Worldwide all point to the single EN URL. This dilutes relevance scores and inflates CPA to €11.73. Separate UK-specific and US-specific campaigns with proper geo exclusions, and route non-English markets to their local-language pages. The EN page ranks #1 organically for "carly discount code" — paid should complement this, not cannibalize it with mismatched geos.
Rec 03
Launch ES with a structured SEO-first approach, then paid
ES has no keyword rankings, no paid presence, and only 18 GA4 sessions. Before committing paid budget, invest in on-page SEO: target "código promocional Carly" and "descuento Carly" which carry purchase intent. Build internal links from DE/EN voucher pages to the ES URL. Once the page establishes organic baseline rankings (3–6 months), layer in a small Performance Max test. The €11.98 CPA from the one ES session suggests conversion mechanics work — the gap is pure traffic.
