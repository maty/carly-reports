Observations & recommendations

  • Demand Gen has no incrementality evidence after 7 months. $187K spent across 19 campaign variants, blended 0.53x ROAS, no acquisition-focused campaign has crossed breakeven.
  • The account's most efficient channel is being starved to fund the least efficient one. Search Lost IS (budget) rose ~4x from Jan–Feb to Mar–Jul while Search ROAS stayed 9.5–14x — 15 to 30 times better than Demand Gen in the same months.
  • Performance Max efficiency is eroding under a flat budget cap. ROAS declined from a 5.60x peak in March to 3.69x in July while spend stayed capped near $60K/month.
  • A Conversion Lift Study has been proposed by Google to test incrementality, but Demand Gen DE's current volume (~103 conversions/28 days) is far below the 1,000+ threshold Google states is needed for significance — the study is not yet feasible on the channel that actually needs validating.
  • Recommendation: set a protected budget floor for Search before any further Demand Gen scaling, and require a statistically valid incrementality read (via the lift study, once volume is addressed) before increasing Demand Gen budget further.
